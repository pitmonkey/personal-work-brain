# People

`Person` and `Org` concepts — the map of who owns what, who to ask about which
system, how a team makes decisions, and how a vendor behaves in practice.

**What belongs here:** what someone owns, what they care about, how they decide,
what they have said publicly, and the working context that would take months to
rebuild if you moved teams.

**What does not:** judgements about a person you would not say to their face, and
anything HR, performance, or compensation related. The test in
[CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) is simple — if a note would embarrass you if read aloud
to its subject, it does not go in.

Prefer one dense page per team or vendor over a thin page per individual.
