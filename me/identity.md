## What this page is for

The minimum viable context file. If an agent could read only one page in this
store, this is it: who you are, what you do, and the handful of facts that make
every other interaction land correctly. Everything else in `me/` elaborates on
this one.

## Interview protocol

*Hand this page to an agent and say "let's do this one." The agent runs the
interview below.*

**Instructions for the agent.** Ask **one question at a time** — never compound
questions, never a list to answer at once. When an answer is vague, push for a
concrete example rather than accepting the abstraction. Do not editorialize or
compliment. When you have enough, say so and draft. 5–8 questions.

**Questions:**

1. What's your role, and who do you work for?
2. What do you actually spend your time on — not the job description, the real week?
3. What are you responsible for that nobody else is?
4. What's your background — how did you end up doing this?
5. What should an agent know about you within the first ten seconds of working with you?
6. What do people consistently get wrong about your role or what you do?

**After drafting:** present it and ask what's wrong. If they say it looks good
with no changes, ask them to name the weakest or most generic sentence and fix
that. **One push, not two.**
