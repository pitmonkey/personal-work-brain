# Meetings

Most meetings live inline in the [daily note](../daily/index.md) under a `###` heading. A meeting earns its own file here when it is long, recurring, or produced decisions worth finding again.

## Shape

- File: `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`, frontmatter `type: Meeting` and `timestamp`. Start from [the template](../templates/meeting.md).
- First line of the body links back to the day: `Daily note: [2026-10-25](../daily/2026-10-25.md)`.
- The daily note keeps a one-line link where the meeting used to be.
- A choice made in the meeting becomes a [`Decision`](../decisions/index.md); the meeting page links it rather than restating it.
